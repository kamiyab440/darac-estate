About Project

Spacious Villas Designed For Comfortable Family Living, Surrounded By Open Green Landscapes In The Heart Of Himmatnagar

Tulsi Villa Two Is A Gated Community Of Independent Villas Planned Around A Central Green Spine. Every Home Is Oriented For Cross Ventilation And Morning Light, With Private Parking, A Landscaped Setback And A Terrace Built For Evenings Outdoors.

The Project Is Delivered Complete: Internal Roads, Street Lighting, Underground Drainage, Rainwater Harvesting And A Perimeter Wall Were Handed Over With The Homes, Not Promised For Later.
