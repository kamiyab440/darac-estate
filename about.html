Our Story

A Local Developer Building For The Way Himatnagar Actually Lives.

Darac Estate started from a simple observation — a town growing this quickly deserves buildings planned with the same care as the cities it is being compared to.

Every project since has followed the same order of work. Understand the land, plan around the family or business that will use it, and only then draw. Architecture, interiors and construction sit with one team, so nothing is handed over the fence and nothing gets value-engineered out at the last minute.

With our residential development now taking shape, that approach is becoming finished streets — homes designed for comfort, connectivity and long-term value, handed over on the date we committed to.

Our Design Build Process

Darac Group Operates As A Full-Service Design-Build Firm, Meaning Architecture, Interior Design, And Construction Are Handled By One Integrated Team.

Architecture Planning And Interior Design

Our Architects Develop Detailed Plans Designed To Maximize Space, Natural Light, And Connectivity. With The Surrounding Landscape, Interior Design Is Brought In Early So Architecture And Interiors Work As One Not As An Afterthought.

Discovery & Vision

The Foundation Of Every DARAC Development Begins With Discovery. We Take The Time To Understand Each Client's Goals, Site Potential, And Long-Term Vision Before A Single Detail Is Finalized.

Construction & Final Completion
